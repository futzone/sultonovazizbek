Frequently Asked Questions

How does the quantum interface work?
Our interface connects directly to quantum backends via a secure API. It visualizes the state vector returned from the QPU in real-time, allowing you to see the effects of gates and noise models instantly.
What quantum hardware is supported?
We support all major superconducting and trapped-ion backends, including IBM Quantum, Rigetti, and IonQ. Our abstraction layer handles the transpilation automatically.
Is prior quantum computing experience required?
While helpful, it's not strictly necessary. Our "Visual Mode" allows you to build circuits using drag-and-drop components and see the results without writing a single line of code.
How secure is the platform?
Security is paramount. We use end-to-end encryption for all job submissions and results. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and offer on-premise deployment for highly sensitive workloads.
